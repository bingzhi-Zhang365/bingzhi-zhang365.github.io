Quantum information · Quantum computing

Bingzhi Zhang（张炳志）

Postdoctoral Research Associate
Princeton University

I study how quantum systems learn, evolve, and process information. My research interests span quantum information and quantum computing, with a particular focus on quantum dynamics, quantum machine learning, and quantum sensing.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Princeton University, working with Hakan E. Türeci. Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Scholar Research Associate at the University of Southern California (USC) from 2024 to 2026, where I also held a Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation postdoctoral fellowship in 2026. I received my Ph.D. in Physics from USC in 2024, advised by Quntao Zhuang.

Research directions

I

Quantum dynamics

Understanding the flow of information, the growth of correlations, and the emergence of collective behavior in complex quantum systems.

II

Quantum neural networks

Exploring the principles, capabilities, and limitations of quantum algorithms and learning, and how the structure of quantum systems can be harnessed for computation.

III

Quantum sensing & networks

Understanding how quantum resources can enhance measurement and communication, and how these advantages can be realized across interconnected quantum systems.
